# Full description (max 4000)
Apex Performance is the app for clients and coaches of the Apex Performance centre. Your trainings, appointments, progress and chat with your coach are always at hand, right on your phone.

FOR CLIENTS
• Trainings – every training with its exercises and sets (reps × kg, time, distance) and your coach's notes.
• Progress – charts of max weight, volume and reps, trainings per week and the share of sets per muscle group.
• Personal records – recorded automatically from your completed trainings.
• Body measurements – weight and circumferences with a chart of how they change, plus FMS movement assessments.
• Appointments – book an appointment, join an existing one or request a cancellation with a comment.
• My package – how many trainings are done and how many are left.
• Goal and plan, and your coach's monthly review of your progress.
• Chat with your coach – messages, images and shared workouts.

FOR COACHES
• Appointment calendar and approving requests with one tap.
• A new training starts as soon as you save it and its time is measured automatically; mark a training for later as planned.
• Training templates, copying trainings and the client's previous sets right next to the input.
• Workout library with videos, categories (mobility and flexibility / strength and conditioning) and a map of the muscles each workout works.
• Client profile: trainings first, progress, body measurements, FMS, goal and plan, monthly reviews and credits.
• Client list with search and a filter by plan.

NOTIFICATIONS
Push notifications about new requests, appointment changes, new trainings, measurements and messages.

The app is available in Croatian, English, Italian and German.

An account opened by the Apex Performance centre is required to use the app.
