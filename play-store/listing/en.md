# Full description (max 4000)
Apex Performance is the app for clients and coaches of the Apex Performance centre. Book sessions, track your progress and browse workouts at any time, right from your phone.

FOR CLIENTS
• Book a session – pick a day, a coach and a free time slot, or join an existing session.
• Send a cancelation request with a note for your coach.
• Follow the status of your requests: approved, pending or declined.
• See your body measurements and a chart of your weight over time.
• Your profile always shows your remaining credits and your training plan.

FOR COACHES
• A calendar of all appointments with a day-by-day overview.
• Approve new appointments, cancelation and join requests with a single tap.
• Generate next week's schedule from recurring appointments.
• Client list with search, plan filter and a badge for clients who are out of credits.
• Client profile: contact details, plan, credits, body measurements with a progress chart and FMS (Functional Movement Screen) assessments.
• Workout library with videos, workout types and import from an Excel template.

NOTIFICATIONS
Push notifications let you know about new requests, appointment changes and new measurements.

The app is available in English, Croatian and Italian.

An account created by the Apex Performance centre is required to use the app.
